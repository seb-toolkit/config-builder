What can Safe Exam Browser see on my computer?

Published 2026-11-26 · Unofficial guide for students

Students often worry about installing exam software. This page explains what Safe Exam Browser (SEB) does on your computer, what it does not do, and what could be recorded. The exact answers depend on how your university set up your exam, so there is also a list of questions to ask them.

Your university’s policies come first. This is general information, not a statement about your university’s system.

What SEB does

  • It is a web browser that fills your screen and opens your exam.
  • During the exam it blocks other programs, other websites and some keyboard shortcuts, as the exam settings say.
  • It can stop screenshots, screen sharing and copy and paste to other programs.
  • It can show your university’s exam system that you are using SEB with the right settings.

What SEB does not normally do

  • It is not a general “spy” program. It runs while you are in the exam and ends when you quit.
  • By default it does not read your files, your photos, your emails or your browsing history.
  • By default it does not turn on your webcam or microphone. That would need separate proctoring software, which you would be told about.
  • It does not change your computer permanently. You can uninstall it like any other program.

What could be recorded

This depends on your university’s setup, so treat the list as possibilities, not promises.

  • The exam system’s records. Moodle and similar systems keep logs of attempts: when you signed in, how you moved through the quiz, and your answers.
  • SEB’s own log. SEB can keep a log on your computer, which you or your university can use to diagnose problems.
  • An exam server. Some universities use an exam server that receives status messages, such as whether your computer is connected or about to run out of battery. Some can also take screenshots and record the active program or window title. If yours does, you should be told in advance.
  • A webcam or invigilator. If your exam is supervised remotely, you will be told.

What to ask your university

  • What is recorded during the exam, and who can see it?
  • How long is it kept, and where?
  • Is there a webcam, microphone or screen recording, and what happens to it?
  • What can I use instead if I cannot or do not wish to install SEB on my own computer?

Universities that record data about you have to tell you what they collect and why (your data-protection notice). Ask the exam office or the data-protection team.

Practical tips

  • Install SEB only from the official sources.
  • If you do not want to install it on a personal computer, ask early about using a university computer.
  • Uninstall it after your exams if you do not need it. If you have another exam later, keep it and update it.
