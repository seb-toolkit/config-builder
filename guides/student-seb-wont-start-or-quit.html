Safe Exam Browser won’t start, won’t quit or has frozen: what to do

Published 2026-11-26 · Unofficial guide for students

Most Safe Exam Browser (SEB) problems have a simple cause. The golden rule: if something goes wrong during an exam, tell your invigilator or exam contact before you do anything drastic. Do not switch the computer off or restart it unless you are told to.

Your university’s instructions come first. Settings vary between exams, so these steps are general. Where they differ from your instructions, follow your instructions.

SEB won’t start

  1. Start it the way you were told. For Moodle exams that normally means opening the quiz and using the Launch Safe Exam Browser button. Double-clicking SEB on its own may open an empty window or an error.
  2. Read the message. SEB often tells you what is wrong. Common causes are in the next steps.
  3. Close other programs. Screen sharing, remote-desktop and screen-recording tools, virtual machines and some chat or overlay programs can stop SEB starting. Save your work, close them, and try again.
  4. Check your version. If the exam asks for a minimum SEB version, an old copy will be refused. Install the latest from safeexambrowser.org or your university’s link.
  5. Check your network. A message about a server or connection means SEB or the exam site cannot be reached. Test another website in your normal browser, move closer to the router or switch network.
  6. Restart SEB, then the computer, if you are doing this before the exam and have time. A fresh start clears many faults.
  7. Still stuck? Take a screenshot or photograph of the message with your phone and contact the IT help desk or exam office. The exact words help them a great deal.

I started the exam, but the page won’t load

  • Wait a minute. Exam sites can be slow when many students start together.
  • Try the page’s own reload button or key, in case your instructions say you can.
  • If the page still will not load, tell the invigilator at once. Say the time and what you see.

SEB has frozen

  • Wait about a minute, since a heavy page can look frozen.
  • Do not press lots of keys or force the computer off. Put your hand up or contact the exam office.
  • If you are told to close and reopen SEB, re-launch the quiz from Moodle. Moodle normally saves your answers as you go and lets you carry on with the attempt that is still open, but how the timer is treated is your university’s decision, so ask.

SEB won’t let me quit

That is deliberate. Depending on how the exam is set up, you leave in one of these ways:

  • An exit button on the quiz page. Some Moodle exams show Exit Safe Exam Browser once you have finished your attempt. It does not appear before that.
  • The quit button in SEB, which asks for a quit password. The invigilator gives you the password at the end. Do not share it.
  • Ctrl+Q on Windows or Cmd+Q on a Mac, which also asks for the password when one is set.

If none of these works, tell the invigilator. Do not try to end SEB by other means before the exam has finished and you have been told you can leave.

It asks for a password I don’t know

  • A quit password is given by your invigilator or lecturer. You will not be able to find it online, and it may be different for each exam.
  • A password to sign in (your university account) is your own. See signing in to Microsoft inside SEB.

What to tell the invigilator or help desk

  • Your name, the exam and the time the problem started.
  • What you were doing and what you saw. The exact wording of any message helps.
  • Your computer type (Windows, Mac or iPad) and SEB version, if you know it.
  • A photo of the screen, if you can take one.

Prevent it next time

Run through the student checklist, and do a practice quiz in SEB if there is one.
