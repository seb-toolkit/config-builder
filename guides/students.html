Safe Exam Browser: help for students

Sitting an exam in Safe Exam Browser (SEB)? These short guides help you get ready, and tell you what to do if something goes wrong.

Your university’s instructions come first. Every exam is set up differently.

  • Before your Safe Exam Browser exam: a student checklistInstall it, test it, and know who to ask, so exam day goes smoothly.
  • Safe Exam Browser won’t start, won’t quit or has frozen: what to doWon’t open, won’t close, asks for a password or has frozen: calm steps for each.
  • Signing in to Microsoft inside Safe Exam Browser: what students need to knowWhy Microsoft sign-in can behave differently in SEB, and how to be ready.
  • Cheating in a Safe Exam Browser exam: the risks to youWhat is really at stake, how cheating gets noticed, and what to do if you are struggling.
  • What can Safe Exam Browser see on my computer?What SEB does, what it does not do, and what your university may record.
  • Download Safe Exam Browser: student downloads and installationOfficial links for Windows, Mac and iPad, and how to install and check it.

This is an independent guide, not from your university or the Safe Exam Browser project. Settings differ between exams, so your lecturer’s or exam office’s instructions always come first.
