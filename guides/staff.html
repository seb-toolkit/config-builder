Safe Exam Browser: guides and downloads for staff

For lecturers, learning technologists and exam teams setting up Safe Exam Browser (SEB) for Moodle.

Open the SEB Config Builder to make the settings file and printable sheets. Students have their own guides.

  • Downloading and uploading files in Safe Exam Browser: where files goWhere downloaded files are saved, what students can pick when uploading, and how to keep it tidy.

  • Preventing access to generative AI in Safe Exam BrowserWhat actually keeps AI assistants out of an exam, and what does not.
  • SEB Server: logging student activity, where to host it, and what happens behind a firewallWhat SEB can record, how it connects to SEB Server, where the server can run, and what fails when the network does.
  • What Safe Exam Browser can and cannot protectA threat-by-threat table: what SEB stops, what it only slows, and what is out of its reach.
  • How to do a test run of your Safe Exam Browser examTest as a student, on every computer type, with a go or no-go checklist before exam day.
  • Downloads for staff: Safe Exam Browser, SEB Server and toolsOfficial SEB downloads, SEB Server and Moodle plugin, plus the builder and printable sheets.
  • When one student cannot use Safe Exam Browser: what the invigilator can doFix it, swap the computer, or run one student with manual invigilation, with two Moodle ways tested.
  • Taking students straight into the Moodle quiz in Safe Exam Browser, with no site menusWhich Moodle pages show the site menu, which do not, and how to close the gaps with the URL filter.
